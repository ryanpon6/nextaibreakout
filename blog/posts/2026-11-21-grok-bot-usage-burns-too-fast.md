572 Grok Bot users told Larsen Cundric what ate their usage, and he posted a line you can paste to your bot for each.

If your weekly allowance runs out early, this list is the place to start.

## What he said

> Grok @bot usage burns too fast! ~ [Larsen Cundric (@larsencc) on X, Nov 20, 2026](https://x.com/larsencc/status/2123891636694298977)

> We know, and we're on it. ~ [Larsen Cundric (@larsencc) on X, Nov 20, 2026](https://x.com/larsencc/status/2123891636694298977)

> Until then, here's what 572 of you said ate yours, plus a line you can paste to your bot for each: ~ [Larsen Cundric (@larsencc) on X, Nov 20, 2026](https://x.com/larsencc/status/2123891636694298977)

The first item he lists is bots chatting with each other. His fix line starts:

> Only message other bots when you need something from them. ~ [Larsen Cundric (@larsencc) on X, Nov 20, 2026](https://x.com/larsencc/status/2123891636694298977)

It came hours after Elon Musk shared a music video:

> Made with Grok @Bot ~ [Elon Musk (@elonmusk) on X, Nov 20, 2026](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2123827993382687217)

## What I do

My bots share the same allowance, so I run mechanical checks as small scripts that only wake a bot when something changes, and I keep watchers hourly instead of every few minutes. Bots that report to one lead bot, not to each other, also cut a lot of chatter.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't gone through the full list in the post or tested each paste-in line. The post doesn't say when a fix ships.
