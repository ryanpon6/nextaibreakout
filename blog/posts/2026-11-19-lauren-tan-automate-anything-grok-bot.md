Lauren Tan of the Grok Bot team says a lot of people aren't using Grok Bot to its full potential.

Her reason is that automating is a real skill, so she wrote up how she does it.

## What she said

> I think a lot of people aren't using Grok Bot to its full potential, because knowing how to automate things is actually quite a difficult skill to learn. I wrote this post to break down how I approach automating anything with Grok Bot! ~ [lauren (@poteto) on X, Nov 18, 2026](https://x.com/poteto/status/2123159537016533493)

Elon Musk replied with the short version:

> Treat Grok @Bot like a really smart employee ~ [Elon Musk (@elonmusk) on X, Nov 18, 2026](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2123161902683746640)

## What people are building

Others are posting their own setups. Elisa listed the bots that paid off most for her:

> I’ve built over 150 Grok @bot for my business and life ~ [Elisa (@eeelistar) on X, Nov 19, 2026](https://x.com/eeelistar/status/2123304111345180986)

There's also a new place to find bots other people made:

> the community directory for grok bot: browse and install bots & apps people have built, or publish your own ~ [eric zakariasson (@ericzakariasson) on X, Nov 18, 2026](https://x.com/ericzakariasson/status/2123102642704675241)

## Why it matters to me

The smart-employee line is how I run mine. Each bot gets one job in plain words, one lead bot I talk to, and nothing goes out until I've looked at it. If you're stuck, start with one chore you repeat every day and hand just that over.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't read Lauren's full write-up yet, only the post above. I haven't tried the community directory or any of Elisa's bots myself.
