Grok Bot plans now give 11% more usage, and up to 50% more for routines.

Here's the post from the team:

> You should now see an additional 11% more usage out of your plan, and up to 50% more usage for routines! ~ [Sam Sokolin (@SamSokolin) on X, Nov 26, 2026](https://x.com/SamSokolin/status/2126027973568466949)

## Where the savings come from

The routine harness skips model calls on steps that don't need one:

> Excited to share more soon on how we optimized our routine harness to remove inference requests for simple and repetitive ~ [Sam Sokolin (@SamSokolin) on X, Nov 26, 2026](https://x.com/SamSokolin/status/2126027973568466949)

## What I'd do

Most of my bots run on routines, so the routine side is the one that matters to me. I'd keep the cheap checks at the top of each routine anyway, so a quiet run stops early and the extra room goes to the runs that do real work.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't measured this on my own plan yet. The numbers above come from the team's post on X, not my own usage screen.
