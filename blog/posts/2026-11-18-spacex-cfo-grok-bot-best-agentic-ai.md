SpaceX CFO Bret Johnsen called Grok Bot "probably the best agentic AI solution out there."

He also said people on his own team are already more efficient with it.

## What he said

DogeDesigner posted the quote:

> I hope everyone's trying Grok Bot, it is amazing. It’s probably the best agentic AI solution out there. ~ [DogeDesigner (@cb_doge) on X, Nov 17, 2026](https://x.com/cb_doge/status/2122780502277140508)

He tied it to what's coming next:

> If you look at Grok Bot and the updates that are coming on the Grok model as well, and the coding capability of Cursor ~ [DogeDesigner (@cb_doge) on X, Nov 17, 2026](https://x.com/cb_doge/status/2122780502277140508)

He also said it's already showing up on his own team:

> I'm already seeing on my own team at work that people are able to be more efficient in what they're doing. ~ [Napoleon Bonaparte (@Napoleon__Bon) on X, Nov 18, 2026](https://x.com/Napoleon__Bon/status/2122951536838099171)

## Why it matters to me

A CFO talking up his own company's product isn't a neutral review. The part I care about is his team line, because that matches what I see: the win isn't one clever answer, it's bots that take whole chores off your plate. If you're starting out, give one lead bot a repeat job and put a checker in front of anything that goes out.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't watched the full interview. Everything here comes from the posts above, and I can't see what his team uses Grok Bot for.
