Grok Bot 1.3.0 is rolling out with smarter Slack bots, restock alerts and mobile buy now.

Here's the rollout post:

> smarter slack bots, restock alerts and mobile buy now, automatic teams setup with org controls, a sideways x drafts carousel, the ashby connector, and bcc on email drafts ~ [eric zakariasson (@ericzakariasson) on X, Nov 25, 2026](https://x.com/ericzakariasson/status/2125675144173556124)

## Bots handing work to bots

The other big piece is bots passing tasks to each other:

> Grok Bots can now delegate tasks to other Bots and deliver the results ~ [Mark Kretschmann (@mark_k) on X, Nov 25, 2026](https://x.com/mark_k/status/2125686210152518004)

## Restock alerts

Sold-out items get a watch button:

> Sold out products get a Notify me that watches the seller ~ [Alex (@alexmichael) on X, Nov 25, 2026](https://x.com/alexmichael/status/2125678183332577371)

## What I'd do

I'd try the restock alert first on something I actually want, and let my lead bot hand research to a second bot instead of doing it all in one chat. Anything that spends money stays on ask-first, so the bot finds it and I hit buy.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't tested 1.3.0 myself yet. Everything above comes from what people posted on X, not my own testing.
