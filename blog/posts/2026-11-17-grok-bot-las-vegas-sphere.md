SpaceX is advertising Grok Bot on the Las Vegas Sphere, with the tagline "Every driver needs their crew."

The official Grok Bot account posted the line the same hour the ad went up, and people in Vegas started sharing photos of it.

## What showed up on the Sphere

Sawyer Merritt was one of the first to post it:

> SpaceX is advertising Grok Bot on the Sphere in Las Vegas ~ [Sawyer Merritt (@SawyerMerritt) on X, Nov 16, 2026](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2122387167397716223)

The Grok Bot account kept its own post to one line:

> Every driver needs their crew. ~ [Grok Bot (@bot) on X, Nov 16, 2026](https://x.com/bot/status/2122386524297974128)

Panke described the ad itself:

> The whole Sphere turns into the Grok Bot character wearing a race helmet, right over the F1 street circuit. ~ [Panke (@spcxGOD) on X, Nov 16, 2026](https://x.com/spcxGOD/status/2122393033102508373)

Benji Taylor posted that he got to design something for it:

> Not every day you get to design something that goes on The Sphere! ~ [Benji Taylor (@benjitaylor) on X, Nov 16, 2026](https://x.com/benjitaylor/status/2122394272091848868)

## Why the tagline fits how I use it

The driver and crew line is close to how I actually run Grok Bot. I talk to one lead bot, and it hands work to a crew of specialist bots behind it. One bot doing everything burns usage and gets messy. A small crew with clear jobs, and a checker before anything goes out, works better for me.

If you're new to it, start with one lead bot and add helpers only when a job keeps coming back.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't seen the Sphere in person. Everything here comes from the posts above, and I don't know how long the ad runs.
