Christopher Stanley says Grok Bot found $590.62 in missing Amazon refunds and got support to agree to pay them.

It's a good example of the boring admin work a bot can grind through for you.

## What he said

> I just had Grok @bot go through all of my Amazon orders and look for refunds that were never issued. ~ [Christopher Stanley (@cstanley) on X, Nov 21, 2026](https://x.com/cstanley/status/2124314532126323042)

> It found $590.62 in missing refunds over several years, opened a support chat, talked to customer service, and got them to agree to issue the refunds. ~ [Christopher Stanley (@cstanley) on X, Nov 21, 2026](https://x.com/cstanley/status/2124314532126323042)

Elon Musk shared the same push a few hours earlier:

> Give Grok @Bot your hardest tasks ~ [Elon Musk (@elonmusk) on X, Nov 21, 2026](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124280710747840889)

## What I do

I let my bots read and prepare things, but anything that sends a message, spends money or signs in waits for my OK. For a job like this I'd have the bot list the orders it thinks are short first, check that list myself, then let it open the support chat.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't tried this on my own Amazon account. The post says support agreed to issue the refunds; it doesn't show the money landing, and some replies doubt the result.
