Elon Musk says Grok Bot can research Black Friday deals and check that they're real.

Here's the post:

> Grok Bot can research the best Black Friday deals for you and confirm they’re authentic ~ [Elon Musk (@elonmusk) on X, Nov 24, 2026](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125464682580623467)

## What users are building

One user posted a shopping agent that compares offers against past prices:

> i built an AI shopping agent that checks real prices before you spend your money ~ [Insomnia (@insomnia_vip) on X, Nov 25, 2026](https://x.com/insomnia_vip/status/2125529663439077850)

## What I'd do

I'd give it the list of things I actually want and ask it to watch prices and ping me on a real drop. I keep anything that spends money on ask-first, so the bot finds the deal and I make the purchase.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't run a Black Friday deal hunt with Grok Bot myself. Everything above comes from what Elon and users posted, not my own testing.
