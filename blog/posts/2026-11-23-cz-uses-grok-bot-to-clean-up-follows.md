CZ had Grok Bot clean up the accounts he follows on X, and told it to keep the constructive critics.

It's a good everyday job for a bot: a long list, a simple rule, and a lot of clicking you'd rather not do yourself.

## What he said

> Used Grok Bot to clean up inactive and abusive accounts I follow. Ask it to keep constructive criticism. Not sure how well it worked. ~ [CZ (@cz_binance) on X, Nov 22, 2026](https://x.com/cz_binance/status/2124698685116383430)

Elon Musk replied with one word:

> Cool ~ [Elon Musk (@elonmusk) on X, Nov 22, 2026](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124733560947138910)

## What I do

For a cleanup like this I'd have the bot make the list first and show me who it plans to unfollow and why. I'd skim it, pull anyone I want to keep, then let it do the unfollows.

## What I haven't checked

I haven't run a follow cleanup on my own account. CZ says himself he's not sure how well it worked, and his post doesn't show which accounts were removed or how many.
